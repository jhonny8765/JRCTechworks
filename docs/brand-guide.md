# JRC Techworks — Brand guide (Phase 8)

## Identity

- Name: **JRC Techworks**
- Positioning: Independent, two-person software studio based in the Philippines
- Primary line: **Software that works for you.**
- Core focus: Websites, web applications, custom software, point-of-sale and inventory tools, and practical automation
- Voice: Direct, friendly, clear, capable, and grounded in what visitors can actually see

JRC Techworks should describe the studio accurately: do not claim legal registration,
headcount beyond two, external client relationships, delivery statistics, or client
testimonials that have not been provided and verified by the owner.

The wordmark uses JRC Techworks with the existing framed `[J]` mark. Keep generous
spacing, bright headings, subtle grid lines, clear borders, and the dark backdrop.
Use plain English rather than technical jargon or exaggerated sales promises.

## Portfolio proof

- **SukiSuite** links to https://sukisuite.com/ and uses the real screenshot provided by the owner.
- **Better Yield POS** links to https://better-yield-magpet-pos.pages.dev/ and uses the real screenshot provided by the owner.
- State observed workflows and interface features; avoid invented analytics and outcomes.
- Keep an obvious link to the actual public-facing product on each case study.

## Inquiry experience

Keep direct contact visible through the owner's provided email and social links.
Avoid claiming a response-time guarantee or collecting inquiry data without adding
the appropriate backend and consent treatment.

## Remaining owner input

The second studio member has not yet provided a public name or role. Keep the
current neutral collaborator label until the owner confirms public details.
